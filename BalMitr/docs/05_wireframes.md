# Wireframe Notes (Web Client)
1. **Login** — Split screen: dark green left info panel (`BalMitr`, scope/rule/demo notes) + right demo-login cards & Sign in form.
2. **Home** — Greeting, 4 summary tiles (Children, Measured this month, Inside normal cut-offs, Need attention), "Needs a visit" list with risk pill & status chips, primary actions (`+ Register a child`, `Open the register`).
3. **Children** — Search/filter register list, Register Child modal/form, sync status badge.
4. **Child Profile** — Child header, Risk card with rule IDs & reasons, Measurement entry form (lying/standing default by age), Recharts growth curves with WHO SD lines + trend banner, Food suggestions card (`demo list: needs review by a nutrition expert`), Follow-ups/Visits/Referrals & progress.
5. **Alerts** — Children needing attention sorted High -> Moderate.
6. **Visits** — Due today, Overdue, Done tabs for follow-ups, plus home visits & referrals.
7. **Summary** — Read-only counts by growth status and risk per centre.
8. **Backend & Account** — API health, DB counts, Swagger link, offline sync queue; profile & logout.
